# Anand Institute of Higher Technology (AIHT) — Complete Information

## ABOUT US

Anand Institute of Higher Technology (AIHT) is an autonomous engineering and technology institution located at Kazhipattur, Chennai, Tamil Nadu, along the IT Corridor on Old Mahabalipuram Road (OMR).

AIHT was founded by Kalvi Vallal Shri T. Kalasalingam and provides undergraduate, postgraduate and research-oriented education across engineering, technology and management disciplines.

The institution is located on a sprawling **25-acre campus** with separate blocks for undergraduate and postgraduate programmes. AIHT focuses on technology-oriented education, practical learning, innovation, entrepreneurship and industry interaction.

AIHT is associated with Anna University and operates under the regulatory framework applicable to engineering and management education in India. The college has **TNEA code 1303**.

AIHT also has a **Technology Business Incubator (TBI)**, and MSME has recognized AIHT as a business incubator.

The institution has more than **36 years of educational experience, 200+ teachers, 5,000+ students and 25,000+ alumni** according to the college's admission information.
